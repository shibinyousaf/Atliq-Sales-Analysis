# Atliq Hardwares Sales Analysis  

## 📌 Project Overview  
This project analyzes the sales performance of **Atliq Hardwares**, a company operating in the Indian market. The analysis covers **four years of sales data (2017-2020)** and provides insights into revenue trends, sales quantity, and top-performing customers and products.  

## 🛠 Tools & Technologies Used  
- **SQL**: Data extraction, transformation, and aggregation  
- **Power BI**: Interactive dashboards and visualizations  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)**: Advanced calculations and measures  

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)  
- **Total Sales Quantity**: Overall units sold  
- **Total Revenue**: Total sales revenue generated  
- **Revenue Contribution by Marketplace**: Revenue breakdown by different Indian marketplaces  
- **Sales Quantity by Indian Market**: Units sold across various regions  
- **Revenue Trends**: Year-over-year revenue growth analysis  
- **Top 5 Customers**: Highest revenue-generating customers  
- **Top 5 Products**: Best-selling products

## 📈 Insights & Takeaways  
- The **revenue trend** indicates steady growth from **2017 to 2020**, with notable spikes in specific years.  
- Certain **marketplaces** contribute significantly more to revenue than others.  
- A small group of **top customers** drive a major portion of the sales.  
- **Best-selling products** are consistently high-performing across all years.  

## 🏆 Conclusion  
This analysis provides valuable **business insights** into **sales performance, customer preferences, and market trends**, helping Atliq Hardwares make **data-driven decisions** for future growth.
